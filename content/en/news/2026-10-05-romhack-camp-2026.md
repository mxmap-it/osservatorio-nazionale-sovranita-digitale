## From the official programme

From the abstract published on the [RomHack schedule](https://cfp.romhack.io/romhack-camp-2026/talk/GHQJFC/):

> «MXMap Italia has been established as national observatory of the status of digital Sovereignty of italian public agencies, by analyzing DNS records on the basis of the MXMap software.»

The talk presented the results of the measurement — **22,987 public bodies analysed**, of which **46% run their email on providers under non-EU jurisdiction** and **0% on European providers based outside Italy** — together with the methodology, its origin in upstream research, and a call to contribute to the project.

## What was covered

- **The method, and where it comes from**: MXMap began with David Huser's work on [mxmap.ch](https://mxmap.ch) and grew into the **SecAssure 2026** paper, covering around 15,300 municipalities in Germany, Austria and Switzerland. MXMap Italia is its Italian adaptation.
- **Why jurisdiction beats geography**: the CLOUD Act and FISA 702, and why keeping data in Italy does not change who can be compelled to hand it over.
- **Sovereignty washing**: a critical reading, measure by measure, of how data residency, key management, confidential computing, operator access controls, local partners and separate European entities are offered as answers to a question that is legal, not technical.
- **The national cloud programme**: why consolidating the public sector's scattered datacentres — necessary, and genuinely achieved — is not the same as making them sovereign.
- **What is still missing**: the measurement of public spending on cloud services, year by year, which is the piece we are building now.
