## The figures presented

The talk presented the measurement of **22,922 public bodies** (94% coverage), snapshot of **9 November 2026**:

- **43.3%** — email on providers under non-EU jurisdiction (reachable by the CLOUD Act): 9,915 bodies;
- **50.5%** — Italian providers: 11,581 bodies;
- **0.5%** — European providers based outside Italy: 123 bodies;
- **5.7%** — unclassified: 1,303 bodies.

The talk abstract, written in September, quoted "more than 46%": the figure presented on stage is the more recent one, produced with the updated methodology. The current numbers, recomputed nightly, are on the [homepage](/en/).

## What was covered

- **The method and where it comes from**: MXMap began with David Huser's work on [mxmap.ch](https://mxmap.ch) and grew into the **SecAssure 2026** paper (around 15,300 municipalities in Germany, Austria and Switzerland). MXMap Italia is its Italian adaptation and follows the upstream project rather than forking away from it.
- **Following the money, not just the machines**: what is new since RomHack is the second instrument, on **public spending on cloud services**. From 3,745,883 public contract notices (CIG) since 2024, filtered in published, reproducible stages, come 4,537 contracts of certain cloud spending worth €1.21bn. The share going to non-EU providers is **still a provisional figure**, and was presented as such: we will publish it with its method once it is consolidated.
- **Why jurisdiction beats geography**: the CLOUD Act, FISA 702, and Microsoft's testimony before the French Senate (June 2025): "I cannot guarantee it."
- **The geopolitical layer**: France's SecNumCloud, the immunity criterion removed from EUCS in 2024, and the open letter asking for it to be restored.
- **Sovereignty washing**: data residency, customer-managed keys, confidential computing, operator access control and separate European entities, examined measure by measure and vendor by vendor.
- **The national cloud programme (PSN)** and what we ask public bodies to do: know their own position, put jurisdiction into the risk assessment, write it into the tender.
- **A side project**: the *Europe–China Digital Commons* research observatory ([eu-china-commons.eu](https://eu-china-commons.eu)).
