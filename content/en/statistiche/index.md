Visit statistics for the Observatory's websites, published like everything else: **mxmap.it** (the map and the pages of each public body), **osservatorio.mxmap.it** and **analisi-spesa-ict.mxmap.it**. They tell us whether the work reaches the people it is meant for and which public bodies draw interest, region by region. We measure them without profiling anyone.
